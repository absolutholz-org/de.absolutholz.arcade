# Queens Rules

Queens is an elegant logic puzzle played on an N×N board divided into N distinct colored regions.

## Objective

Place exactly **N crowns** on the board such that:

1. **Rows & Columns:** Every row and every column contains exactly **one crown**.
2. **Colored Regions:** Every colored region contains exactly **one crown**.
3. **King's Adjacency Rule:** No two crowns may touch each other horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

## How to Play

- **Mark (X):** Click or tap an empty square to mark it with an **X** to indicate that no crown can sit there.
- **Crown:** Double-click or switch to Crown Mode to place a crown.
- **Drag to Eliminate:** Click and drag across empty squares to rapidly mark entire rows or zones with X.
- **Auto-Cross:** Enable the Auto-Cross helper to automatically place X marks in the row, column, and 8 neighbor cells whenever you place a crown.
