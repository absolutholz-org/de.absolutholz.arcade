# How to Play Minesweeper

## Objective

Uncover all safe squares across the grid while avoiding hidden mines.

## Numbers

A number on a revealed square shows how many adjacent squares contain mines.

## Flagging

Right-click or press F to place a flag on squares you believe contain a mine.

## Chording

Double-click or middle-click a number when you have placed all adjacent flags to uncover remaining squares quickly.
