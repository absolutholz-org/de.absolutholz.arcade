# Anagram Scramble Rules

Anagram Scramble is a word puzzle. You get a rack of letter tiles and must discover every word you can build from them.

## Objective

Use the tiles in your rack to find valid words. Each tile can be used **once per word**. The board shows blank slots grouped by word length; every word you find fills its slot.

## How to Play

1. Pick a **difficulty** and a **mode**.
2. Select tiles to build a word, then submit it.
3. If the word is valid, it is revealed on the board and you score points. Longer words score more.
4. Clear the round by finding a word that uses **every tile**, or by finding **at least half** of all the words.
5. Find every word for a perfect sweep.

## Difficulty Levels

| Level | Tiles | Shortest word |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Easy | 5 | 3 letters |
| Medium | 6 | 3 letters |
| Hard | 7 | 3 letters |
| Expert | 7 | 4 letters |

## Modes

- **Zen:** No timer. Reveal the answers whenever you like.
- **Timed Arcade:** You start with 2:30 and every word you find adds bonus seconds. You must find a word that uses every tile before time runs out.

## Controls

- **Keyboard:** Type letters to stage matching tiles. `Backspace` recalls the last tile, `Enter` submits, `Space` shuffles the rack and `Escape` clears your word.
- **Mouse or touch:** Select a rack tile to stage it, and select a staged tile to return it to the rack.
