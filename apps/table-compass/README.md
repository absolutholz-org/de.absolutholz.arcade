# Table Compass (`@arcade/table-compass`)

Analog tabletop companion utility tool for the absolutholz Arcade monorepo.

## Features

- **Multi-Touch First-Player Picker**: Place 2–8 fingers on the table to start a 2-second lock-in countdown and determine the starting player or full turn order.
- **Tabletop Seating Geometry**: Captures real physical touch positions and sorts seats clockwise around the central dial.
- **Turn & Direction Compass**: Large, readable compass dial pointing directly at the active player, with single-tap turn advancing, direction reversing, and turn undo.
- **Accessible Fallback**: Full keyboard and single-pointer parity with number stepper and random starter picker for non-touch devices and screen readers.
- **Screen Wake Lock**: Prevents screens lying flat on game tables from turning off mid-game.
- **High-Contrast Design & OKLCH**: 8 distinct geometric shapes paired with OKLCH colors meeting WCAG 2.2 AA / BITV 2.0 standards.
