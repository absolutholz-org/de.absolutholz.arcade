## Objective

**Tic-Tac-Toe** is a turn-based alignment game for two players played on a $3 \times 3$ grid. The objective is to be the first player to align three of your marks ($X$ or $O$) horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

## Basic Rules

1. **Players & Marks:** Player 1 plays as **$X$**, and Player 2 plays as **$O$**.
2. **Turn Alternation:** Players take turns placing their mark in an empty grid cell. Marks cannot be moved or removed once placed.
3. **Victory:** The first player to align three consecutive marks in any row, column, or diagonal immediately wins the round.
4. **Draw (Cat's Game):** If all 9 grid cells are occupied and neither player has achieved three in a row, the match concludes in a draw.
5. **Starting Mark Rotation:** To ensure fairness in local Pass & Play matches, the starting player alternates each round (if $X$ started the first game, $O$ starts the next game).

## Game Modes

- **Pass & Play (Local):** Two players take turns on the same screen or device.
- **Play Online (Coming Soon):** Remote peer-to-peer play with shareable room codes and links.

## Controls & Accessibility

- **Touch & Mouse:** Tap or click any open cell to place your mark.
- **Keyboard Navigation:**
  - `Arrow Keys`: Move focus between grid cells.
  - `Space` or `Enter`: Place your mark on the currently focused cell.
  - `Home` / `End`: Jump to the first or last cell in the active row.
