# Dice Bingo Rules

**Dice Bingo** blends classic two-dice rolling with spatial poker hand construction. Each turn, you roll two standard six-sided dice (2d6) and place their sum into any open, unblocked cell on the grid.

---

## Objective

Build the highest-scoring combination of poker hands across **all rows, columns, and diagonals** before filling every open cell on the board.

* **5×5 Board (Medium & Hard):** Evaluates 12 lines (5 rows, 5 columns, 2 diagonals).
* **4×4 Board (Easy):** Evaluates 10 lines (4 rows, 4 columns, 2 diagonals).
* **Diagonals Score Double (×2):** The main diagonal and anti-diagonal always grant double points!

---

## Hand Scoring Hierarchy

### Standard 5-Number Hands (Medium & Hard)
* **Five of a Kind (10 pts):** All 5 cells contain the identical value (e.g. `[6, 6, 6, 6, 6]`).
* **Straight without a 7 (12 pts):** 5 consecutive numbers with no 7 (e.g. `[2, 3, 4, 5, 6]` or `[8, 9, 10, 11, 12]`, unordered).
* **Straight with a 7 (8 pts):** 5 consecutive numbers containing a 7 (e.g. `[3, 4, 5, 6, 7]` or `[5, 6, 7, 8, 9]`).
* **Full House (8 pts):** Three of one value and two of another (e.g. `[4, 4, 4, 9, 9]`).
* **Four of a Kind (6 pts):** 4 cells with the same value (e.g. `[10, 10, 10, 10, 3]`).
* **Three of a Kind (3 pts):** 3 cells with the same value (e.g. `[5, 5, 5, 2, 8]`).
* **Two Pair (3 pts):** Two distinct pairs (e.g. `[6, 6, 8, 8, 3]`).
* **One Pair (1 pt):** One matching pair (e.g. `[7, 7, 3, 4, 9]`).

*Hand Precedence:* Each line receives the score of its highest matching hand classification.

### Scaled 4-Number Hands (Easy & Obstacle Lines)
* **Four of a Kind (8 pts):** 4 matching numbers.
* **4-Number Straight (6 pts):** 4 consecutive numbers (unordered).
* **Three of a Kind (3 pts):** 3 matching numbers.
* **Two Pair (2 pts):** Two pairs.
* **One Pair (1 pt):** One matching pair.

---

## Difficulty Modes

* **Easy (4×4):** 16 turns on an open 4×4 board with 4-number hand scoring rules.
* **Medium (5×5):** 25 turns on an open 5×5 board with classic poker hand rules.
* **Hard (5×5 with Obstacles):** 23 playable turns on a 5×5 board containing 2 randomly pre-blocked obstacle cells. Lines intersecting obstacles evaluate across their remaining playable cells.

---

## Strategy Tips

1. **Protect the Center:** The center cell `(2, 2)` on a 5×5 grid intersects 4 separate scoring lines (Row 3, Col 3, and both diagonals). Place high-probability sums like 7 wisely!
2. **Commit to Straights Early:** Aiming for Straights without 7 nets 12 base points (or 24 points on a diagonal).
3. **Dump Difficult Rolls:** Use isolated cells or lines you've already abandoned to safely place extreme sums like 2 or 12.
