# Accessibility Statement

We are committed to making our digital arcade accessible and barrier-free for all people, including users with visual, auditory, motor, or cognitive disabilities.

## Conformance Standards

This application is designed to conform strictly with:
- **WCAG 2.2 Level AA** (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines)
- **BITV 2.0** (Barrierefreie-Informationstechnik-Verordnung)

## Core Measures

- **Keyboard Navigation:** Every game and interactive control is completely operable via standard keyboard inputs without keyboard traps.
- **Color Contrast & Modes:** All colors adhere to WCAG 2.2 contrast ratios, offering high-contrast options and system color scheme synchronization.
- **Screen Reader Support:** Proper semantic HTML landmarks, ARIA labels, and live regions keep screen reader users informed during active gameplay.
- **Touch Target Sizes:** Interactive touch targets strictly adhere to minimum dimensions (at least 24×24 CSS pixels).

## Feedback & Contact

If you encounter accessibility barriers or have suggestions for improvement, please contact us at **accessibility@absolutholz.de**.
