# Water Sort Rules

Water Sort is a captivating liquid-sorting logic puzzle. Your mission is to pour colored liquid layers between test tubes until each tube contains exclusively one single color, or remains completely empty.

## Objective

Sort all mixed liquids so that:
- Every non-empty test tube is completely filled with a single, uniform color.
- Empty tubes can be used as temporary sorting buffers during intermediate moves.

## Pouring Mechanics

1. **Selection:** Click or tap a test tube to pick it up as the source.
2. **Pouring:** Click or tap a target test tube to pour the top layer of liquid into it.
3. **Valid Pours:** You may only pour liquid if:
   - The target tube is not full (maximum capacity is 4 liquid units).
   - The target tube is completely empty, **OR** the top liquid layer of the target tube matches the color of the liquid being poured.
4. **Volume Transferred:** A single pour automatically transfers all contiguous top segments of the identical color, up to the remaining capacity of the destination tube.

## Helpful Tips

- **Buffer Tubes:** Use the empty tubes strategically to isolate mismatched colors and free up base layers.
- **Undo & Reset:** If you reach a dead end with no legal pours, use **Undo** (`U`) to step backwards or **Reset** (`R`) to restart the puzzle from scratch.
- **Colorblind Support:** Enable pattern overlays in the settings menu to distinguish liquid segments through accessible geometric motifs.
