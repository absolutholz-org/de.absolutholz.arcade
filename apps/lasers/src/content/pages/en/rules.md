title: How to Play Laser & Mirrors
description: Rules, optics mechanics, additive color mixing, and keyboard controls for Laser & Mirrors.
---

## Objective

Rotate mirrors and beam splitters on the grid so that **every target receiver** is simultaneously illuminated by a laser beam of its matching color.

## Optical Elements

- **Laser Emitter:** Fires a continuous colored beam in a fixed cardinal direction (`N`, `E`, `S`, `W`).
- **Rotatable Mirror (`/` or `\`):** Click or press `Enter`/`Space` to toggle between 45° (`/`) and 135° (`\`), reflecting incoming beams by 90°.
- **Fixed Mirror:** Locked at a preset angle and cannot be rotated.
- **Beam Splitter:** A half-silvered mirror that lets 50% of the incoming beam pass straight through while reflecting 50% at 90°.
- **Opaque Wall:** Absorbs and stops any laser beam hitting it.
- **Target Receiver:** Powers up when struck by a beam matching its exact required color and geometric symbol.

## Additive Color Mixing

On **Hard** and **Expert** grids, multiple primary emitters combine additively whenever their beams merge or strike the same target receiver:

- **Red (`●`) + Blue (`▲`) = Magenta (`◆`)**
- **Red (`●`) + Green (`■`) = Yellow (`★`)**
- **Blue (`▲`) + Green (`■`) = Cyan (`✚`)**

## Controls

- **Mouse / Touch:** Tap any rotatable mirror or splitter to flip its angle.
- **Keyboard:** Use **Arrow keys** to move across the grid, **Space / Enter** to rotate the focused mirror, **Ctrl/Cmd + Z** to undo, **Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + Z** to redo, **H** for a hint, and **P** to pause.
