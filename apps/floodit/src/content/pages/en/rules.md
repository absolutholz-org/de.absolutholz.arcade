# Flood-It Rules

Flood-It is a fast-paced, addictive color-sorting puzzle game where your goal is to conquer the entire board using as few moves as possible.

## Objective

Flood the entire grid with a **single matching color** before reaching the maximum move limit:

1. **Origin:** You always start controlling the cluster originating from the top-left cell `(0, 0)`.
2. **Flooding:** Choose one of the 6 palette colors (different from your current cluster color).
3. **Absorption:** All cells in your controlled cluster instantly turn into the chosen color and immediately absorb any adjacent matching colored cells.
4. **Victory:** As soon as 100% of the grid shares the same color within the move limit, you win!

## Difficulty Tiers

- **Beginner (8×8):** 4 colors, 18 moves limit — Great for young and casual players.
- **Easy (10×10):** 5 colors, 22 moves limit — Relaxed warm-up challenge.
- **Medium (14×14):** 6 colors, 26 moves limit — The standard puzzle challenge.
- **Hard (18×18):** 6 colors, 32 moves limit — A demanding board requiring strategic foresight.

## Controls & Shortcuts

- **Mouse / Touch:** Click or tap any of the 6 color buttons in the control dock.
- **Keyboard Numbers:** Press keys `1` through `6` on your keyboard to instantly select the corresponding color.
- **Undo:** Press the Undo button (or shortcut `Z`) to revert a flood step.
