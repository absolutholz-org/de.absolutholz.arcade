## Objective

Color every region of the map using only **4 colors** (`●` Coral, `▲` Amber, `■` Emerald, `◆` Cobalt) so that **no two bordering regions share the same color**.

1. **Shared Borders Only:** Two regions are adjacent only when they share a horizontal or vertical edge. Regions touching solely at a diagonal corner **may** share the same color.
2. **Locked Starting Clues:** Regions marked with a corner pin/ring are fixed clues that cannot be erased or changed.
3. **Unique Solution:** Every generated map is mathematically verified to have **one unique valid 4-coloring**.

## Controls

### Tap / Click Mode
- Select one of the **4 color swatches**, the **Eraser**, or toggle **Pencil Mode** in the palette bar, then tap or click any unlocked region.

### Classic Drag-to-Copy
- **Copy Color:** Drag from any colored region into an unlocked region to copy its color.
- **Erase Color:** Drag from outside the map border into an unlocked region to clear it.
- **Candidate Pencil Dots:** Right-drag (or drag while **Pencil Mode** is active) from a colored region into an uncolored region to toggle a small candidate dot.

### Keyboard Shortcuts
- **Arrow Keys:** Move focus to neighboring regions.
- **1–4:** Assign a color (or toggle a candidate dot when Pencil Mode is active).
- **Shift + 1–4:** Toggle a candidate dot directly without switching modes.
- **P:** Toggle Pencil Mode.
- **Backspace / Delete / 0:** Erase the focused region.
- **Z / Y:** Undo / Redo.
- **Esc:** Pause game.
